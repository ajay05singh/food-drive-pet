1. Concatenate clips 1–20 in order (`ffmpeg -f concat`), then lay the VO on top.
2. Burn in subtitles (the faceless-channel standard) and add lower-thirds for key numbers (2.25%, "$0 FX fees").
3. Add quiet royalty-free background music at about −22 LUFS under the VO.
4. End card: subscribe button and "Not affiliated with Wealthsimple. Not financial advice."
